## Reportable Misconduct
Employees are expected to report behaviors that harm company integrity, including fraud, financial misconduct, data manipulation, or safety breaches[cite: 1]. Identities of whistleblowers are kept entirely confidential[cite: 1].

## Escalation Matrix
If a workplace issue or operational grievance is raised, it must follow this clear path of resolution[cite: 1]:
1. **First Point of Contact:** Share directly with your local Reporting Manager[cite: 1].
2. **HR Escalation (If unresolved within 15 days):** Contact Ankit Pradhan (Designation: HR Manager) at `Hr@rikalp.in`[cite: 1].
3. **Vigilance Escalation (If unresolved within 1 month):** Contact Ramakant Tank (Designation: Vigilance Officer) at `Vigilance@rikalp.in`[cite: 1].
