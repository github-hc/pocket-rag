## Working Schedules
* **Working Days:** Monday to Saturday. The second Saturday of every month is a designated off day.
* **Office Hours:** 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM. Employees are expected to be at their desks and starting work by 9:30 AM[cite: 1].
* **Lunch Break:** One hour break on a staggered schedule between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM[cite: 1].

## Attendance Logging
* All on-site staff must log their walk-in and walk-out times via the biometric system at the reception[cite: 1]. Field and branch employees must use the Lystloc Mobile Tracking application[cite: 1].
* Failure to log tracking correctly can result in an automatic absent marking for that day[cite: 1].

## Flexibility and Delays
* **Flexi-Entry:** Employees are permitted a flexi-entry buffer window up to two times a month, provided they still clock a full 9 hours for the day[cite: 1]. 
* Failing to meet the 9-hour expectation or defaulting beyond the two-time limit results in a half-day absence marking and disciplinary review[cite: 1].
* Unauthorized absences without reporting manager notification are strictly treated as indiscipline[cite: 1]. For long-term absences exceeding 5 days without intimation, see the termination conditions under the [Leave and Holidays Policy](leave_policy.md)[cite: 1].
