## Earned Leaves (EL)
* Regular employees receive 18 Earned Leaves per calendar year. 
* Mid-year joiners receive a pro-rata allotment based on their start date[cite: 1]:
  * Joining on or before the 10th: 1.5 leaves credited[cite: 1].
  * Joining between the 11th and 18th: 1 leave credited[cite: 1].
  * Joining after the 20th: No leave credit for that month[cite: 1].
* Employees also receive a 0.5 leave credit on their birthday[cite: 1].

## Core Restrictions
* No paid leaves are permitted during the initial 3 months of employment[cite: 1].
* No leaves can be taken while serving a notice period[cite: 1].
* Unintimated absence exceeding 5 consecutive days makes the employment contract liable for immediate termination[cite: 1].
* Unused leaves automatically lapse at the end of the year, with a maximum carry-forward allowance of 6 leaves to the next cycle[cite: 1].

## Casual Leaves (CL)
* Employees are allowed up to 12 days of Casual Leave per calendar year[cite: 1]. 
* CL usage is generally capped at a maximum of 3 consecutive days at one time, unless special relaxation is approved by HR[cite: 1].

## Statutory Maternity Benefits
* Female employees with over 1 year of continuous service qualify for 26 weeks of full-pay maternity leave[cite: 1]. Employees registered under ESIC receive their maternity provisions directly through ESIC[cite: 1].
